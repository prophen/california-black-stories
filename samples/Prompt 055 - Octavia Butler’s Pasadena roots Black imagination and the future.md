## Caption

Before she imagined other worlds, Octavia Butler found room to read in Pasadena. 📚✨

Born in Pasadena in 1947, Butler spent childhood hours at Central Library and later visited La Pintoresca Branch after school. The Black girl who loved those shelves became a science-fiction writer whose work asks difficult questions about power, survival, and change.

Her California story is a reminder to make room for Black imagination—not just to remember where we’ve been, but to consider where we could go. Which Black author changed how you see the future? Tell us the author and the book. 💬
